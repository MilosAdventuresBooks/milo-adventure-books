Milo's Adventures

Picture books by Mia Prinsloo

Gentle picture books for ages 1 to 5 about Milo, his Ouma and Dog. Every book is in English, with an Afrikaans translation (and sometimes French too): a gentle first taste of another language for any family.

The books

  • Boo, Hoo, Rawr! / Boe, Hoe, Rawr!A gently spooky, never scary first Halloween. Ages 1 to 5.
  • So Much to Be Thankful ForA cosy first gratitude story in English, Afrikaans and French. Ages 1 to 5.
  • Snowy Christmas, Sunny ChristmasA Christmas story for families with someone they love far away. Ages 2 to 6.
  • First Snow! / Eerste Sneeu!A toddler's very first snow, with Ouma and Dog. Ages 1 to 5.
  • Wie's daar? / Who's There?Garden animal sounds, Afrikaans first with English underneath. Babies to age 5.
