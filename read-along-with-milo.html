Read along with Milo

Lees saam met Milo

First readers to read together, in English and Afrikaans. Ten books in order, each one building on the one before.

How the books work

Made for reading together

  • Read together. A grown-up reads first, sliding a finger under the words, and the child joins in. A short guide inside each book shows three easy steps: look and talk, read along, then read it yourself. Lees saam. 'n Grootmens lees eerste en skuif 'n vinger onder die woorde, en die kind lees saam. 'n Kort gids in elke boek wys drie maklike stappe: kyk en gesels, lees saam, en lees dan self.
  • Use them with phonics. These are look-and-see readers: children learn whole words by sight, helped by the pictures. Use them together with the phonics your child learns at school. Gebruik hulle saam met klanke. Dit is kyk-en-sien-leesboeke: kinders leer hele woorde op sig ken, met die hulp van die prente. Gebruik hulle saam met die klanke wat jou kind by die skool leer.
  • Again, together, with help. Learning words by sight takes repetition, a grown-up who shows how, and support along the way. Read the same book many times: it is made to be read again and again. Show how reading works by pointing to each word as you say it and reading with expression. When your child gets stuck, say the word, point to the picture and praise every try. Weer, saam, met hulp. Om woorde op sig te leer ken, vra herhaling, 'n grootmens wat wys hoe, en hulp langs die pad. Lees dieselfde boek baie keer: dit is gemaak om oor en oor gelees te word. Wys hoe lees werk: wys na elke woord terwyl jy dit sê, en lees met uitdrukking. As jou kind vassit, sê die woord, wys na die prent en prys elke poging.
  • Read them in order. Each book builds on the one before. The words your child already knows come back, and a few new ones join them. Start with Book 1. Lees hulle in volgorde. Elke boek bou voort op die vorige een. Die woorde wat jou kind al ken, kom terug, en 'n paar nuwes kom by. Begin met Boek 1.
  • Two languages. English is on the left page and Afrikaans on the right. Read one language or both. Twee tale. Engels is op die linkerbladsy en Afrikaans op die regterbladsy. Lees een taal of albei.
  • Fun at the back. Sight words and a picture word list, then activities to practise reading and writing: match, trace, colour, count, find the words, a maze and a story to put in order. Answers for grown-ups, and a certificate to cut out, with a star to colour each time your child reads the book. Pret agter in die boek. Sigwoorde en 'n prentwoordelys, dan aktiwiteite om lees en skryf te oefen: pas, oortrek, inkleur, tel, soek die woorde, 'n doolhof en 'n storie om in volgorde te sit. Antwoorde vir grootmense, en 'n sertifikaat om uit te knip, met 'n ster om in te kleur elke keer as jou kind die boek lees.

English on the left page, Afrikaans on the right

Where is Milo's ball? His red ball is gone! Milo asks Mom, Dad and Grandma, and everyone looks and looks. A first reader for reading together: 14 new words, each one in the pictures, used again and again, and listed at the back in English and Afrikaans. Dog is in every picture. Can you find him?

Waar is Milo se bal? Sy rooi bal is weg! Milo vra vir Mamma, Pappa en Ouma, en almal soek en soek. 'n Eerste leesboek om saam te lees: 12 nuwe woorde, elkeen in die prente, wat weer en weer terugkom en agter in die boek in Engels en Afrikaans staan. Hond is in elke prent. Kan jy hom vind?

Would you like a book in another language?

Every Milo picture book is in English and Afrikaans. If your family would love one in another language, or another pair of languages, send an email to mia.prinsloo.books+requests@gmail.com with the book and the language you would like.

We read every request and will consider each one, though we can't promise every language.
