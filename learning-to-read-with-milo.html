My Ball Is Gone! / My bal is weg!

English on the left page, Afrikaans on the right

Where is Milo's ball? His red ball is gone! Milo asks Mom, Dad and Grandma, and everyone looks and looks. A first reader for reading together: 14 new words, each one in the pictures, used again and again, and listed at the back in English and Afrikaans. Dog is on every page. Can you find him?

Waar is Milo se bal? Sy rooi bal is weg! Milo vra vir Mamma, Pappa en Ouma, en almal soek en soek. 'n Eerste leesboek om saam te lees: 12 nuwe woorde, elkeen in die prente, wat weer en weer terugkom en agter in die boek in Engels en Afrikaans staan. Hond is op elke bladsy. Kan jy hom vind?

Milo in your language

Would you like a book in another language?

Every Milo picture book is in English and Afrikaans. If your family would love one in another language, or another pair of languages, send an email to mia.prinsloo.books+requests@gmail.com with the book and the language you would like.

We read every request and will consider each one, though we can't promise every language.

All the books are paperbacks. Each button opens Books2Read in a new tab, which takes you to your own country's Amazon. It may say "Ebook" and show a Kindle button: tap it and it opens the paperback.
