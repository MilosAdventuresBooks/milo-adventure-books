Privacy Policy

Milo's Adventures and Milo Social Uploader. Last updated 19 October 2026.

Who we are

Milo's Adventures is a series of picture books by Mia Prinsloo. Milo Social Uploader is a small private tool the author uses to upload and schedule short videos about the books on the author's own Milo's Adventures YouTube channel.

What the uploader does

The uploader signs in to the author's own Google account with Google's permission screen and uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What data is collected

None from visitors or viewers. The uploader stores one sign-in token for the author's own account, privately on the author's computer, so that scheduled videos can be uploaded. It does not read, collect, sell or share anyone's personal information, and it does not use YouTube data for advertising.

This website has no sign-up forms, no comments and no tracking or analytics of its own.

Removing access

The author can remove the uploader's access at any time in Google Account > Security > Third-party access. Google's own use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a message to @milo.adventure.books on Instagram.
