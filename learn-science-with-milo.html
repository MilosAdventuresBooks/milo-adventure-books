Big questions, answered with Milo: real science for curious little minds, in English and Afrikaans. More science books are on the way.

Space

What's Up There? / Wat is daar bo?

English first, with Afrikaans underneath

Look up! What's up there? Milo looks up at the big blue sky and wonders: what's up there? With some cardboard, a roll of tape and a whole lot of imagination, he and Dog are about to find out. Three, two, one... blast off! Zoom past the Moon, the Sun, the planets, the stars and even more!

Kyk op! Wat is daar bo? Milo kyk op na die groot blou lug en wonder: wat is daar bo? Met 'n bietjie karton, 'n rol kleefband en 'n hele klomp verbeelding gaan hy en Hond dit uitvind. Drie, twee, een... en weg is hulle! Vlieg na die maan, die son, die planete, die sterre en nog veel meer!

Milo in your language

Would you like a book in another language?

Every Milo picture book is in English and Afrikaans. If your family would love one in another language, or another pair of languages, send an email to mia.prinsloo.books+requests@gmail.com with the book and the language you would like.

We read every request and will consider each one, though we can't promise every language.

All the books are paperbacks. Each button opens Books2Read in a new tab, which takes you to your own country's Amazon. It may say "Ebook" and show a Kindle button: tap it and it opens the paperback.
